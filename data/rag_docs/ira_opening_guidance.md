# Fidelity IRA Opening Guidance

## When a Fidelity IRA must be opened first
A 401(k)-to-IRA rollover requires a destination Fidelity IRA to exist before the
rollover can be initiated. If the customer does not already hold a Fidelity IRA,
the associate should guide the customer through opening one as the first step.

## What the associate should confirm before opening
- The customer does not already have a suitable existing Fidelity IRA (check the
  customer's retirement accounts first to avoid opening a duplicate).
- The IRA type aligns with the source plan and the customer's stated intent
  (for example, a Traditional IRA for pre-tax 401(k) balances). The associate
  confirms the customer's stated intent and does not recommend a specific
  product as a personalized investment recommendation.
- The customer's identity verification is complete.

## Sequencing
1. Confirm no suitable IRA exists.
2. Open the appropriate Fidelity IRA with the customer.
3. Only then initiate the rollover request from the source plan into the new IRA.

## Notes
- Opening the IRA is an account-servicing action, not an investment
  recommendation. Keep language neutral and avoid suggesting specific funds or
  allocations.
- If the customer already holds a Fidelity IRA, opening a new one is usually
  unnecessary; confirm whether the existing IRA is the intended destination.
