# Required Forms and Documents — Rollover

## Source plan paperwork
The former employer's plan (the source 401(k)) typically requires its own
distribution or rollover-out paperwork. The exact form is issued by the source
plan provider. The associate confirms the provider and helps the customer
request the correct source-plan paperwork.

## Fidelity IRA application
If the customer does not already hold a suitable Fidelity IRA, a Fidelity IRA
application must be completed and the account opened before the rollover.

## Rollover request form
A Fidelity rollover request form directs the incoming funds into the destination
IRA. This is completed once the destination IRA exists.

## Identity verification / document requirements
Identity verification must be complete before servicing proceeds. Standard
document requirements include government-issued identification and confirmation
of the customer's current address on file. If identity verification is missing
or incomplete, the case should be paused and escalated rather than proceeding.

## Typical checklist for a standard 401(k)-to-IRA rollover
- [ ] Identity verification complete
- [ ] Fidelity IRA opened (or confirmed existing)
- [ ] Source-plan rollover/distribution paperwork requested
- [ ] Fidelity rollover request form completed
- [ ] Source-plan provider and approximate balance confirmed

## Notes
Missing application or rollover-request forms are normal early-stage items the
associate helps the customer complete. Missing identity verification, missing
source-plan details, or an account restriction are different — those should be
escalated (see Escalation Policy).
