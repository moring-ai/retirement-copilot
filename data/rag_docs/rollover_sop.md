# 401(k)-to-IRA Rollover — Standard Operating Procedure

## Overview
This procedure covers moving assets from a former employer's 401(k) plan into a
Fidelity IRA. It applies to associate-assisted servicing cases. The associate
guides the customer through the process; the associate does not move funds or
execute trades on the customer's behalf inside this workflow.

## Direct rollover vs. indirect rollover
A **direct rollover** moves funds straight from the source plan to the Fidelity
IRA without the money being paid to the customer. It is the standard recommended
operational path because it avoids mandatory withholding and the 60-day
re-deposit requirement. A **indirect rollover** pays the funds to the customer
first; the customer then has 60 days to deposit the amount into the IRA, and the
source plan may apply mandatory withholding. Indirect rollovers carry more
operational and timing risk and should be flagged for closer review.

## High-level steps
1. Confirm the customer's identity and locate the customer profile.
2. Confirm whether the customer already holds a Fidelity IRA. If not, an IRA
   must be opened before the rollover can be initiated.
3. Confirm the source plan details: provider, plan type, approximate balance,
   and whether the plan permits a rollover (rollover eligibility).
4. Check for account restrictions or holds that would block servicing.
5. Confirm which forms and documents are required and which are still missing.
6. Prepare the source-plan paperwork and the Fidelity rollover request.
7. Once the IRA exists and paperwork is complete, the rollover request can be
   submitted through the standard channel.

## Operational notes
- Prefer a direct rollover unless the customer's situation specifically requires
  otherwise; route indirect rollovers for additional review.
- Do not promise a specific tax outcome; tax treatment depends on the customer's
  individual circumstances (see Tax Advice Boundaries).
- Eligibility is confirmed only when complete source-plan details are available.
  If plan details are incomplete or unavailable, do not state the rollover is
  guaranteed to be allowed.
